Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that combines information retrieval with large language models. It first retrieves relevant documents from a knowledge base, then feeds those documents as context to the language model to generate a grounded answer. RAG reduces hallucinations and improves factual accuracy.

FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search) is a library for efficient similarity search and clustering of dense vectors. It is commonly used in RAG pipelines to store and retrieve document embeddings.
